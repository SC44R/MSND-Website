Act I, Scene I Before the Curtain

The Story

Shakespeare's A Midsummer Night's Dream is about four young lovers' complicated relationships become entangled in the plots of the fairy king and queen, Oberon and Titania, in this magical forest outside Athens. When Hermia and Lysander elope to the woods, Demetrius (father's choice, Hermia's love) and Helena (Helena's love, Demetrius's-choice) go after them. With the help of his sidekick Puck, Oberon uses a magic flower to influence the affections of the four lovers and Titania falls in love with a weaver named Bottom, whose head has been enchanted by Puck into the shape of a donkey's head.

At the very end of the play, Puck sorts out the confusion of the magic, sorts out the lovers (Lysander with Hermia, Demetrius with Helena) and brings about reconciliation between Titania and Oberon. A triple wedding in Athens and a farcical performance by Bottom and his fellow amateur actors conclude the story, leaving readers with a witty reflection on the irrationality of love and the indistinguishability of dreams and reality.
